# 🎬 Netflix Movies & TV Shows Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs an Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on the Netflix Movies and TV Shows dataset using Python. The objective is to uncover meaningful insights about Netflix's content library, including content distribution, release trends, ratings, genres, countries, directors, and more.

This project is a part of my **21 Days Data Analytics Challenge**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing
- Handle missing values and duplicate records
- Explore Netflix content using visualizations
- Identify trends in movies and TV shows
- Generate business insights from the dataset

---

## 📂 Dataset

- **Dataset Name:** Netflix Movies and TV Shows
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Records:** 8,800+ Titles
- **Format:** CSV

## 📊 Key Insights

- Movies significantly outnumber TV Shows on Netflix.
- United States contributes the highest number of titles.
- TV-MA is the most common content rating.
- Drama and International Movies dominate the catalog.
- Netflix experienced rapid content growth after 2015.
- Most movies have durations between 80–120 minutes.
- Most TV Shows contain only one season.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

- Increase investment in high-performing genres such as Drama and International Movies.
- Expand children's content in underrepresented regions.
- Continue producing popular mature-rated content while maintaining a balanced content library.
- Invest in countries with growing content demand to increase global reach.
- Analyze viewer preferences to optimize future content acquisition.
